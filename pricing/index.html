Plans FAQs

How is pricing determined?

We quote deployments based on monthly traffic/usage, catalog depth, logic complexity, and any required integrations. This keeps pricing aligned to real scope — and ensures you’re not paying for features you don’t need.

What’s included in setup?

Setup typically includes brand styling, catalog ingestion or configuration, symptom-to-product mapping, QA testing, and a launch-ready embed (iframe or widget). Advanced deployments may include additional logic layers or integrations.

How fast can we go live?

Pilot deployments can go live quickly once we have your product data and branding assets. Growth and Enterprise rollouts depend on catalog depth, integration needs, and desired customization.

Can we start small and expand later?

Yes. Many brands begin with a Pilot on one funnel or storefront, then expand into Growth once the impact is proven. We can also scope a phased rollout from day one.

Do you recommend only our products?

For brand deployments, recommendations can be locked to your catalog. If you want a broader “educational” mode, we can scope that separately depending on your compliance and positioning preferences.
